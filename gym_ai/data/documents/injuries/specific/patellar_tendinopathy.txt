DOCUMENT_TITLE: Patellar Tendinopathy (Jumper's Knee)
CATEGORY: Rehabilitation & Modification

1. MECHANISM OF INJURY:
- Overload of the patellar tendon due to high-impact jumping, sprinting, or heavy knee-dominant lifting (excessive forward knee travel under extreme load).

2. CONTRAINDICATED MOVEMENTS:
- Deep knee flexion exercises that cause sharp pain (e.g., heavy Ass-To-Grass squats, plyometric jumps).
- Heavy leg extensions emphasizing the bottom stretch position.

3. EXERCISE MODIFICATIONS & REHAB:
- Phase 1: Isometric holds (e.g., Spanish Squats, Wall Sits for 30-45 seconds, 4-5 sets) to reduce pain.
- Phase 2: Heavy Slow Resistance (HSR) training. 3-4 seconds eccentric, 3-4 seconds concentric on Leg Press or Hack Squat, keeping pain below 3/10. Avoid explosive concentric movements.
