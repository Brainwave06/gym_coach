DOCUMENT_TITLE: Distal Biceps Tendinopathy and Elbow Strain
CATEGORY: Rehabilitation & Modification

1. MECHANISM OF INJURY:
- Acute or chronic strain typically occurs during heavy eccentric loading combined with forearm supination (e.g., heavy barbell curls, reverse-grip pull-ups, deadlift mixed grip).

2. CONTRAINDICATED MOVEMENTS (Avoid During Recovery):
- Heavy loaded supination (Underhand/Reverse grips).
- Deep eccentric stretching of the elbow flexors under load (e.g., Preacher Curls at the bottom position).

3. EXERCISE MODIFICATIONS (Safe Alternatives):
- Shift to Neutral Grip (Hammer grip) or Pronated Grip (Overhand) for all pulling movements.
- Use Neutral-Grip Lat Pulldowns or Assisted Machine Rows to significantly reduce torque on the distal biceps insertion point.
- Implement isometric holds at the mid-range of motion before reintroducing concentric loads.
