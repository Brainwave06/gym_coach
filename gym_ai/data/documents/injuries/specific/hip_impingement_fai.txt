DOCUMENT_TITLE: Femoroacetabular Impingement (FAI) and Groin Pain
CATEGORY: Rehabilitation & Modification

1. MECHANISM OF INJURY:
- Bone-on-bone friction at the hip joint, typically felt as a "pinching" sensation in the deep groin at the bottom of a deep squat or leg press. Often exacerbated by a narrow stance.

2. CONTRAINDICATED MOVEMENTS:
- Deep squats (past parallel) with a narrow stance.
- High-box step-ups causing extreme hip flexion.

3. EXERCISE MODIFICATIONS:
- Widen the squat stance and externally rotate the toes (15-30 degrees) to open the hip capsule.
- Limit Depth: Use Box Squats or Pin Squats to physically restrict range of motion just above the point of impingement.
- Emphasize hip-hinge movements (RDLs, Glute Bridges) over deep squat patterns.
