DOCUMENT_TITLE: Wrist Pain and TFCC Injury Management
CATEGORY: Rehabilitation & Modification

1. MECHANISM OF INJURY:
- Triangular Fibrocartilage Complex (TFCC) strain occurs from heavy loading with the wrist in extreme extension (e.g., catching a heavy power clean, or bench pressing with the bar resting high in the fingers instead of the palm).

2. CONTRAINDICATED MOVEMENTS:
- Heavy Barbell Bench Press with extended wrists.
- Barbell Bicep Curls with a straight bar (forces unnatural wrist supination).

3. EXERCISE MODIFICATIONS:
- Use wrist wraps to cast the joint into a neutral, stacked position during all pressing movements.
- Replace straight barbell curls with EZ-Bar Curls or Dumbbell Hammer Curls.
- Switch to neutral grip for pressing (e.g., Dumbbell Floor Press with palms facing each other) to eliminate wrist extension.
