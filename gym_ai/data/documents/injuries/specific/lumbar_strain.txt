DOCUMENT_TITLE: Lumbar Strain and Lower Back Management
CATEGORY: Rehabilitation & Modification

1. MECHANISM OF INJURY:
- Excessive spinal flexion under load (e.g., "butt wink" during deep squats, or rounded lower back during deadlifts).

2. CONTRAINDICATED MOVEMENTS (Acute Phase):
- Barbell Deadlifts (Conventional or Sumo).
- Heavy Barbell Back Squats.
- Seated or unsupported bent-over rows.

3. EXERCISE MODIFICATIONS:
- De-load the spine completely: Leg Press, Hack Squats, or Bulgarian Split Squats.
- Replace bent-over barbell rows with Chest-Supported Rows or Cable Rows.
- Strengthen core bracing capacity (McGill Big 3: Bird-Dog, Modified Curl-up, Side Plank) rather than spinal flexion exercises (avoid sit-ups).
