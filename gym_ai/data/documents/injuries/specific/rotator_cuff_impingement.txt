DOCUMENT_TITLE: Rotator Cuff Impingement and Shoulder Pain
CATEGORY: Rehabilitation & Modification

1. MECHANISM OF INJURY:
- Compression of the supraspinatus tendon or subacromial bursa. Often caused by excessive overhead pressing with poor thoracic mobility, or heavy lateral raises with internal rotation ("pouring water" technique).

2. CONTRAINDICATED MOVEMENTS:
- Behind-the-neck presses or pulldowns.
- Upright rows with a narrow grip.
- Bench pressing with elbows flared out at 90 degrees.

3. EXERCISE MODIFICATIONS:
- Press in the "Scapular Plane" (elbows tucked roughly 30-45 degrees relative to the torso).
- Replace barbell pressing with neutral-grip dumbbell pressing.
- Focus on scapular stabilizers: Face Pulls, Band Pull-Aparts, and external rotation drills.
