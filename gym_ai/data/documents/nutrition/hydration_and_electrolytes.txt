DOCUMENT_TITLE: Hydration Kinetics and Electrolyte Balance
CATEGORY: Sports Nutrition

1. HYDRATION TARGETS:
- Baseline: 35-45 mL per kg of body weight daily.
- Performance penalty: A loss of >2% of body weight in water induces a significant drop in aerobic capacity and muscular endurance.

2. ELECTROLYTE REPLACEMENT (Intra-Workout):
- Sodium (Na+): The primary electrolyte lost in sweat. Target 500-1000 mg of sodium per hour of intense, sweat-inducing exercise (especially in hot climates).
- Potassium (K+): 150-300 mg per hour.
- Osmolality: Isotonic sports drinks (6-8% carbohydrate solution) provide the fastest gastric emptying and intestinal absorption rates.
