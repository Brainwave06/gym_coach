DOCUMENT_TITLE: Gastrointestinal (GI) Distress in Athletes
CATEGORY: Clinical Nutrition

1. CAUSES OF INTRA-WORKOUT GI DISTRESS:
- Training shifts blood flow away from the splanchnic (digestive) bed to working muscles.
- High fiber, high fat, or hyperosmolar (highly concentrated sugar) meals consumed within 2 hours pre-workout delay gastric emptying, causing nausea and bloating.

2. MANAGEMENT & MODIFICATIONS:
- Pre-workout meals should focus on low-FODMAP, easily digestible carbs (e.g., rice cakes, ripe bananas).
- For protein powder bloat: Switch from Whey Concentrate to Whey Isolate (lactose-free), or try Hydrolyzed Whey which is pre-digested.
