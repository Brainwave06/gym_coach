DOCUMENT_TITLE: Mechanisms of Hypertrophy and Training Volume
CATEGORY: Biomechanics & Training

1. HYPERTROPHY MECHANISMS:
- Mechanical Tension (Primary Driver): Achieved by lifting heavy loads through a full Range of Motion (ROM).
- Metabolic Stress: Achieved through higher reps and restricted blood flow.
- Muscle Damage: A byproduct, not a primary goal.

2. TRAINING VOLUME TARGETS (Per Muscle Group/Week):
- Maintenance Volume (MV): 4-6 sets.
- Minimum Effective Volume (MEV): 8-10 sets.
- Maximum Adaptive Volume (MAV): 12-20 sets.
- Maximum Recoverable Volume (MRV): Typically 20+ sets, beyond which performance degrades.

3. INTENSITY (RPE/RIR):
- Sets should be taken to 1-3 Reps in Reserve (RIR) or an RPE of 7-9 to ensure sufficient motor unit recruitment.
