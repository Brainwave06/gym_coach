DOCUMENT_TITLE: Progressive Overload and Load Management
CATEGORY: Training Methodology

1. PROGRESSIVE OVERLOAD METHODS:
- Load Progression: Increasing weight on the bar while maintaining form.
- Rep Progression: Performing more repetitions with the same weight.
- Set Progression: Adding working sets.
- Technical Progression: Improving form or increasing Time Under Tension (TUT).

2. DOUBLE PROGRESSION MODEL:
- Select a rep range (e.g., 8-12 reps). Use a fixed weight until you can hit the top of the range (12 reps) for all sets. Once achieved, increase the weight by 2.5-5% and build reps back up from 8.

3. EXERCISE SELECTION:
- Compound Movements: Squats, Deadlifts, Bench Presses. High systemic fatigue, foundation for overall mass.
- Isolation Movements: Curls, Extensions. Low systemic fatigue, targeted muscle failure.
