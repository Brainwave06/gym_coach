DOCUMENT_TITLE: Evidence-Based Supplements (Creatine, Protein, Caffeine)
CATEGORY: Ergogenic Aids

1. CREATINE MONOHYDRATE:
- Purpose: Increases ATP regeneration for anaerobic performance.
- Dosage: 3 to 5g/day consistently. Loading phase (20g/day for 5-7 days) is optional for faster saturation. Safe for renal function.

2. PROTEIN POWDERS:
- Whey Isolate: >90% protein, near-zero fat/lactose. Fast absorption (ideal post-workout).
- Whey Concentrate: 70-80% protein, contains some lactose. Economical.
- Micellar Casein: Slow-digesting (6-8 hours). Ideal before fasting or sleep.

3. CAFFEINE ANHYDROUS:
- Dosage: 3.0 to 6.0 mg/kg consumed 45-60 mins pre-workout. Blunts perceived exertion (RPE). Tolerance builds rapidly; requires cycling.
