DOCUMENT_TITLE: Macronutrient Targets and MPS Kinetics
CATEGORY: Sports Nutrition

1. PROTEIN REQUIREMENTS:
- Standard Target: 1.6 to 2.2 g/kg of total body weight daily.
- Extreme Deficit Target: 2.3 to 3.1 g/kg of Lean Body Mass (LBM) daily.
- Meal Distribution: 3 to 5 protein-rich meals spaced 3-5 hours apart. Minimum of 2.5g-3.0g of Leucine per meal to trigger Muscle Protein Synthesis (MPS).

2. CARBOHYDRATE REQUIREMENTS:
- General Strength Training: 3.0 to 7.0 g/kg/day. 
- Post-workout resynthesis is accelerated by high GI carbs within 2-4 hours post-exercise.

3. FAT REQUIREMENTS:
- Minimum threshold for hormonal health (Testosterone): 0.6 to 1.0 g/kg/day (or 20-30% of total daily calories). Saturated fats should be kept below 10%.
